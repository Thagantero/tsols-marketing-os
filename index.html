Common questions

What can clients see?

Authorized clients can view their own content, calendar, leads, advertising, approved reports, business profile and package.

Do clients need a ChatGPT account?

No. Select Sign in and use your TSOLS account. You’ll go straight to your client portal.

How do I get access to the client portal?

Ask your Thagana Solutions administrator for a client invitation, then sign in using the invited email address.

Which time zone does TSOLS Marketing OS use?

The workspace uses East Africa Time (Africa/Nairobi) for its calendar and reporting workflows.
